V2 CHANGES
==========

- Implements the conservative U.S.-origin definition agreed with Brett:
  both education and employment histories must be present; every record must
  have a valid country; and every country must be the United States.
- Leaves incomplete histories unassigned in the preferred definition.
- Preserves a broader robustness definition that assigns all workers with no
  observed non-U.S. signal to the United States.
- Adds diagnostic flags for history presence, country completeness, and
  unresolved non-U.S. signals.
- Uses a new project and output directory; no V1 or final-linkage files are
  overwritten.
